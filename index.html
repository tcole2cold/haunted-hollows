The Haunted Hollows Backyard Ultra

What?

  • This is a new, invitation-only backyard ultra near Pittsburgh. Participants will be running a two mile loop, twice an hour, until they either give up or ten hours have passed.
  • Afterwards, we’ll be camping in the Haunted Hollows. Hopefully our running efforts will appease the spirits enough to allow us a safe night’s rest.
  • We are limiting this race to 20 runners. They are welcome to bring loved ones to spectate and hang out. Maybe there will be more runners in a future version of this race. Maybe none will survive year one.
  • You must complete two loops an hour to stay in the race. Extra loops beyond the two do not count for anything.
  • If there is more than one runner left for the tenth hour, then the first runner to finish both of that hour’s loops is the winner.

When?

  • Our run will begin at 8 a.m. on Saturday, October 24, 2026.
  • It will last (at most) 10 hours.

Where?

  • The location of this race is a non-disclosed, private, wooded property in the Coraopolis area.
  • To view the loop and the exact location, please begin filling out the Google Form in the How section below. You’ll need the password.
  • There is ample room for us all to camp after. If you want to camp, plan on bringing your own provisions and camping gear.
  • The course itself is a trail that winds through woods and some fields. It is very runnable and ends in a good climb. It is mostly single track.
